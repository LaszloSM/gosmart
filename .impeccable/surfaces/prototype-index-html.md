# Surface brief: GoSmart prototipo (pasajero + conductor)

Scope: single-file clickable prototype of the GoSmart passenger and driver apps for Riohacha–Maicao, inside a phone frame, with a presenter rail (role switch, screen index, scenario triggers). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience/job: passenger plans and rides a chained Riohacha–Maicao trip knowing the corridor state; driver manages availability, occupancy, payment key, ride closing, income history. Also shown at a university defense, so every module of the proposal must be findable in seconds.

Constraints: all data synthetic and labeled; no people/communities identified in reports; dispatch shown as a range; Colombian Spanish; no em-dashes; light-first (sun), dark tokens provided.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GoSmart as a sunlit street object from Riohacha centro, flat limewashed walls of color that announce the state of the road, refusing the category's dark map-dashboard with a neon accent (the incumbent look).

OWN-WORLD: cool lime-white ground, deep indigo ink, Caribbean turquoise as the calm/primary wall, ochre as caution, coral as blockade. Color is applied as whole painted fields, not scattered accents. Archivo in condensed heavy caps for rótulos (destinations, states, fares) like hand-painted sign lettering; Archivo regular width for UI text. Phosphor icons. One radius family (14px panels, pill chips), tinted soft shadows only on floating sheets.

STORY: the passenger sees whether the Troncal is open before anything else, says one sentence, watches the chat repaint into a three-leg itinerary with one price, pays once, rides with live state, survives a blockade by detour, and keeps one receipt. The driver sees demand, fills seats, gets paid to their own key, and builds a verifiable history.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the home screen's top half is one painted wall in the corridor's current state color with the state as a large condensed rótulo ("TRONCAL ABIERTA" / "BLOQUEO KM 42"), route and last-confirmed time beneath; directly below, the assistant input "¿Para dónde vas?" as the primary action; then one anticipatory suggestion and quick places; bottom nav.

FORM: candidate 7 of 7 (Riohacha centro limewash facades and malecón), seed key ad542ef6, degraded roll (no challengers). Signature interaction: the wall repaint, a clip-path wipe in the new state color when corridor state changes and when the chat becomes the itinerary.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
